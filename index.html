Privacy Policy — Panchang

Published by VyotLabs · Last updated: 27 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Panchang app ("the app") handles your information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Summary

The app has no user accounts, shows no ads, and contains no analytics or tracking SDKs. It does not sell or share your personal data. The daily panchang is computed entirely on your device. A few optional astrology features (Kundali, Gun Milan, Graha Gochar) send the birth details you enter to our own calculation server to produce the result, as described below.

Location

If you grant location permission, the app reads your device location only to calculate the panchang (sunrise, sunset, tithi, nakshatra, kaals, etc.) for your place and to look up your city name. Your location is used on the device and is not stored on any server for the panchang. If you deny the permission, the app uses a city you pick and everything else works normally.

Astrology features and our calculation server

The optional Kundali, Gun Milan and Graha Gochar features require an internet connection. When you use them, the app sends the inputs you provide — the date, time, and the coordinates and time zone of the birth place you select — to our calculation service at panchang.tsingh-devops.click so it can compute planetary positions, charts and compatibility scores. This service is stateless: it uses the inputs only to compute and return the result, and does not store them, link them to your identity, or share them with any third party. We do not collect your name, email or any account information with these requests.

Notifications

If you enable the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. No notification data leaves your device.

Data the app does NOT collect

The app does not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, files, or account information, and does not use advertising identifiers.

Data storage

App preferences (chosen language, region, reminder setting, last-used location) are stored locally on your device only. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.

For questions about this policy, contact vyotlabs@gmail.com.
